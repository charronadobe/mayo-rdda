      International Patient Services at Mayo Clinic

      Mayo Clinic International Patient Services helps patients and families traveling from outside the U.S. understand their care options and plan what comes next. Explore Mayo Clinic specialties and locations, learn how the appointment process works, review cost and insurance information, or contact us for help before you travel.

      Get care at Mayo Clinic

      Mayo Clinic specialists care for serious, rare or complex conditions across many medical and surgical specialties. International patients receive care at Mayo Clinic locations in Arizona, Florida and Minnesota.

      Understand the appointment process

      Learn how to request an appointment, what information you may need to provide and what happens after Mayo Clinic reviews your request. You can also learn about costs, insurance and payment options as you plan for care.

      Get support for your visit

      Mayo Clinic International Patient Services can help with travel planning, language services, appointment logistics and other practical needs before, during and after your visit. Some countries and regions also have Mayo Clinic Representative Offices that can provide local support.

      Why patients choose Mayo Clinic

      • Top ranked in more specialties than any other hospital
      • A team of experts focused on your care
      • Research that advances care for serious or complex conditions
      • Support and practical advice from others visiting Mayo Clinic

      Next steps for international patients

      If you are ready to request an appointment, review the international patient appointment process. If you have questions or are not sure where to start, contact Mayo Clinic International Patient Services.
